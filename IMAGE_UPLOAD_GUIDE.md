## How to Add Images

1. Save each photo from our conversation to your computer
2. Rename them according to the filenames above
3. Place them in the `public/images/gallery/` directory
4. The gallery will automatically display them!

## Alternative: Quick Setup

If you want to use different filenames, just update `src/lib/data.ts` in the `galleryItems` array to match your chosen filenames.
